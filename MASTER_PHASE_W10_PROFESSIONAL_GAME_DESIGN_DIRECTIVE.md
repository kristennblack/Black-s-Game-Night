## Why W.10 exists

W.9 added much-needed detail to Prop Hunt character modeling and controls, but the project still had an append-only prompt structure and too many qualitative phrases such as `better controls`, `more 3D` and `more realistic` that could be interpreted in different ways.

W.10 converts those ambitions into a production framework.

## W.10 professional upgrades

1. One explicit source-of-truth precedence table.
2. Product north star and six experience pillars.
3. Player personas that include non-gamers, children and phone-first family play.
4. Quality priority ladder that puts playability, rules, camera and fairness before decoration.
5. John/Papa's Shop vertical slice as a hard gate before character/map propagation.
6. Action-based, context-sensitive input design for hunter/hider/mobile/desktop/gamepad.
7. Accessibility/comfort settings including sensitivity, invert axes, sprint modes, camera shake, haptics, aim assist and touch-layout presets.
8. Touch ergonomics based on modern platform guidance.
9. WebGL/mobile performance budgets and significance tiers.
10. Explicit Prop Hunt state machine and information-privacy contract.
11. Professional map blockout, landmark, routing, prop-ecology and randomization rules.
12. Detailed shot pipeline that keeps crosshair, muzzle, tracer, impact and hit validation aligned.
13. Character-production workflow with five-view proof before `approvedModel: true`.
14. Local QA telemetry for camera, stuck, mantle, transform, shot and frame-time failures.
15. First-time, stress and real-family playtest methods.
16. Production scorecard and clear Definition of Done by feature type.
17. Cold-ZIP release discipline and visual proof bundle.
18. Explicit list of forbidden shortcuts that previously created misleading progress.

## Highest priority next milestone

W.10 does not authorize broad 3D expansion yet.

The flagship milestone is one production-quality John in Papa's Shop that proves, on an actual phone:
- approved likeness;
- correct hands/rig;
- close stable shoulder camera;
- responsive movement and mantle;
- visible two-hand prop-zapper;
- crosshair/tracer/impact alignment;
- safe hider transformation/decoy/flash/lock;
- stable multiplayer state;
- acceptable sustained mobile performance.

Only after this slice passes should the system propagate to the rest of the approved family cast.
