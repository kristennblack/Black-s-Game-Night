# W.18 Phone / Tablet Acceptance Pass

Use this as the short real-device release gate.

## Backgammon
- Roll/open game, tap a glowing checker, tap a glowing destination.
- Confirm checker moves immediately.
- Confirm LEGAL MOVES fallback works.

## Blackgammon
- Reach four-distinct-single allocation.
- Tap two dice and confirm allocation.
- Also test a KEEP X + Y fallback button.
- Make a checker move and test direct move fallback.

## Deck Sweep
- With cards still in hand, play one of the four exposed front cards.
- Play a same-rank hand + exposed-table combination.

## Campfire Chaos
- Resolve a pending Take 4.
- Confirm four cards arrive and the next turn becomes playable without reload.

## Golf
- Reject a stock draw with 2+ face-down own cards: must choose one to flip.
- Reject visible discard with 2+ face-down own cards: same behavior.
- With one face-down card left, reject a draw and confirm that last card stays hidden.

## Mexican Train
- Confirm the community train is distinct from all player trains.
- Confirm your train is easy to identify.
- Play a double and confirm the next play must close it.

## Trail Trouble
- Complete at least three turns using normal board controls.
- Complete one turn with Quick Move fallback.

## Prairie Pots
- Complete at least three turns.
- Use the direct PLAY fallback once.

## Family Mystery
- Roll, tap a final reachable glowing clue block and watch the standee walk the route.
- Enter a room and confirm close room view returns cleanly.
- Test Camper <-> Living secret passage.
- Test Shop <-> Papa's Shop secret passage.
- Check room art and Cabin-style realism on portrait and landscape screens.

## Molly's Light Chase
- Test arrow/WASD.
- Test swipe.
- Test on-screen direction buttons.
- Catch enough lights to visibly grow the trail and increase speed.
- Hit wall and own trail to confirm run-over behavior.
- Confirm Neon Snake does not appear as a separate shelf game.
