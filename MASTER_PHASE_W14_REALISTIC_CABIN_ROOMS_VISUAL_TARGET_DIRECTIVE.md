Lock the cabin rooms, catalog, and visit-the-cabin overview to a polished, realistic, premium visual target based on the approved realistic mockups.

3. At least one furnished room reads as realistic, cozy, and complete.
4. Catalog thumbnails are large, clear, and appealing.
5. Mobile decorator UI remains touch-friendly and visually clean.
6. Materials read as wood, fabric, stone, leather, glass, and metal appropriately.
7. Lighting is warm and premium rather than flat or cold.
