## Marbles & Jokers

The Phase H board, recessed-hole and marble treatments are implemented in the application's HTML/CSS renderer. They are not generated proof images substituted for the game. The included `MARBLES_ACTUAL_*_PROOF.png` files were captured from the development renderer for QA comparison.

## Prop Hunt

The package retains the Phase G GLBs for John, Papa's Shop + barn and the Papa prop set. They pass technical GLB audits. Their source is project-local algorithmic asset generation rather than a Blender/Maya studio asset pipeline. They have not passed the user's final John-likeness and environment-art approval gate.

## Reference and proof images

Approved family and environment references remain design targets. Concept previews and AI-generated target images are excluded from the clean staging ZIP so they cannot be mistaken for running-game proof.
