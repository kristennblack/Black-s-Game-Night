BLACK FAMILY GAME NIGHT v1.1.0-tryout.3

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS TRYOUT
- The real v1.0.10 Lodge, lobby, room, avatar, chat and 18 existing multiplayer games remain in place.
- Family Mystery retains the illustrated room scenes, family avatar standees, glowing reachable movement blocks, animated pawn travel, suggestions, disproving, detective notes and accusations from tryout.2.
- Family Prop Hunt now launches a detailed third-person software-3D gameplay slice instead of the earlier top-down prototype.
- The 3D engine uses real X/Y/Z world state, perspective camera projection, obstacle heights, jump gravity, landing/support calculations and climbable geometry.
- Papa's Shop is the hero 3D map with shop walls, barn connector, workbench, tool chest, shelving, climbable tractor, old motorcycle, lumber, fireplace, Papa's yellow chair, pen fences, pallets and believable shop clutter.
- Camper/Campsite, Backyard + Fire Pit and Farmyard/Animal Pens use the same 3D movement engine and include their own vertical routes such as bunks, picnic table, truck, trampoline, trailers, hot tub, sea cans, goat stairs and goat platforms.
- Human players are visible third-person family characters using the real family picture assets for their faces plus the locked outfit directions. Dogs render as playable dog characters.
- Hunters visibly hold a stylized prop-zapper and aim with the third-person crosshair.
- Hiders transform into the same 3D prop renderer used for ordinary environment props, so a locked disguised prop does not look alive.
- Damage now produces sparks; the third hit produces the catch/poof flow.
- Mobile controls include movement joystick, drag-to-look, Shoot, Jump, Prop, Flash, Decoy and Lock/Unlock.
- Computer controls include WASD, mouse drag camera, left click shoot, Space jump, E prop, C decoy, Q flash, X lock and R reload.
- Empty seats in the Prop Hunt tryout are filled by computer players with Easy, Normal or Hard difficulty.

IMPORTANT
The new 3D Prop Hunt mode is still a gameplay slice for testing look, movement and map feel. It is local/solo with computer-fill in this tryout. The proven 18 existing games keep the original multiplayer Worker code untouched. Full live multiplayer synchronization for the real-time 3D mode is a later integration step after the movement/camera/map feel is approved.
