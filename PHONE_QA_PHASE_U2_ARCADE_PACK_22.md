# Phone QA - Phase U.2 Family Arcade Pack 22

Test on the same phone/browser used for the existing game-night app.

## Arcade Corner
- Confirm Arcade Corner shows 14 games.
- Confirm each new card opens its own game and Back to Lodge returns home.
- Confirm Share Link works for each new arcade card.
- Confirm no card causes horizontal page scrolling.

## Camp Pong
- Touch/mouse paddle follows accurately.
- Left/right keys work.
- Edge vs center paddle hits clearly change ball angle.
- Papa Bot is beatable but not idle.
- First to 7 ends and rematch restarts cleanly.

## Goat Crossing
- Swipe and keyboard movement both work.
- Road vehicles cost one life on collision.
- Goat is carried by moving logs in water.
- Missing a log costs one life.
- Reaching the hay increases score/level and restarts the crossing.

## Shop Bomber
- Touch D-pad is comfortable.
- Charge button places a charge without double taps.
- Blasts stop at permanent walls and break crates.
- Critters can be cleared by blasts.
- Player can be caught by a blast and loses a life.
- Clearing all critters advances the room.

## Cabin Blocks
- Touch buttons do not misfire adjacent controls.
- Keyboard movement/rotation/drop works.
- Pieces cannot pass through the floor or settled blocks.
- Full rows clear.
- Rotation near walls remains usable.
- Game-over and restart work.

## Camp 2048
- Swipes register only one direction per gesture.
- Matching tiles merge once per move.
- New tile appears only after a valid move.
- Score/best persist.
- No-move game over restarts.

## Minefield
- First reveal is never a mine and its immediate neighboring area is safe.
- Tap reveals.
- Hold flags on phone without accidentally revealing.
- Right-click flags on desktop.
- Empty cells flood correctly.
- Win/loss overlays work.

## Goat Whack
- Targets are easy to tap without being too generous.
- Combo rises on successful consecutive hits.
- Red toolbox subtracts score/resets combo.
- Run ends at 30 seconds.
- High score persists.

## Memory Mayhem
- Only two unmatched cards are open at once.
- Mismatch stays visible long enough to read, then flips back.
- Matches remain revealed.
- Moves/time update correctly.
- Completion overlay and reshuffle work.

## Firelight Simon
- All four stones are large enough for thumb input.
- Each stone has a distinct visible glow and tone.
- Sequence playback cannot be accidentally interrupted by taps.
- Correct sequence advances round.
- Wrong input ends run.
- Best round persists.

## Papa's Pipes
- Tap rotates exactly one tile per tap.
- Lit network visibly propagates from source.
- New puzzle is solvable.
- Whole network completion triggers victory.
- Next job creates a different scrambled network.

## Global pass/fail
Do not mark the phase visually approved until all ten games are readable in portrait phone view, touch controls feel reliable, no canvas is clipped, restart loops are clean, and the Arcade Corner remains easy to browse.
