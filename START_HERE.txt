STAGING BUILD: GAME-NIGHT-STAGING-PHASE-I-THREE-GAMES-07
PACKAGE VERSION: 3.0.1-staging-phase-i-three-games-07

PURPOSE
This is the first staging package that includes Mexican Train, Skip-Bo and Backgammon inside the existing Black Family Game Night app. It is intended for real-device gameplay and visual QA before production replacement.

NEW GAME 1: MEXICAN TRAIN
- 2–8 players/bots
- complete Double-12 set
- 15 / 12 / 11 tile rack sizes based on player count
- private racks and boneyard
- engine/highest-double opening
- private trains, open trains and shared Family Train
- family-avatar open-train markers
- forced double closure
- one-tile warning
- three-round, low-total scoring
- complete score sheet

NEW GAME 2: SKIP-BO
- 2–6 players/bots
- 162-card original visual deck
- Stock size setting: 10 / 20 / 30
- private five-card hand and future Stock cards
- public Stock top/count and four public Discard Piles
- four central Building Piles
- draw to five and mid-turn refill
- play from hand, Stock or Discard top
- Wild values, 1–12 completion and Draw-Pile recycling
- clear Stock to win

NEW GAME 3: BACKGAMMON
- 2 players
- standard 15-checker setup
- full legal turn sequence enforcement
- both-dice, larger-die and doubles rules
- hits, Bar, forced re-entry and bearing off
- normal/gammon/backgammon scoring
- doubling cube and optional rule settings
- dimensional board, checkers, dice, cup and cube
