BLACK FAMILY GAME NIGHT v1.6.0-prop-mystery-test

PROP HUNT + FAMILY MYSTERY TEST BUILD

UPLOAD
1. Keep the hidden .git folder in your existing project checkout.
2. Replace the tracked project files with this package.
3. Commit and push to the same branch Cloudflare currently deploys.
4. Let Cloudflare deploy normally.
5. On phones, refresh once after deployment if an older service-worker shell is still visible.

TEST PROP HUNT FIRST
- Pick a family character and avatar style.
- Add computer players and choose their character/style/difficulty.
- Start as a hider and confirm the left joystick moves continuously while held/dragged.
- Test the D-pad backup, Jump, Sprint and Camera Reset.
- Drag the main play area to rotate/look around.
- Transform with PROP and compare your disguise to the same scenery object.
- Lock the prop and confirm the disguise stops moving while the camera still moves.
- Try Flash and Decoy.
- Jump/climb onto low scenery and try the larger climbable routes.
- Try all four maps: Papa's Shop, Camper / Campsite, Backyard + Fire Pit, Farmyard / Animal Pens.

TEST FAMILY MYSTERY
- Start with one human plus computer detectives if desired.
- Roll and confirm glowing legal destinations show gold movement-cost numbers.
- Pan/zoom the board and use FIT and ME.
- On a phone, confirm the sticky turn-action bar stays easy to reach.
- Enter a room and make a suggestion.
- Confirm the suggested family character is pulled into the room.
- Confirm Case History shows the suggestion/disprover but never names the private card shown.
- Check the grouped notebook: Suspects / Objects / Locations.
- Try a secret shortcut/passsage when available.
- Try a final accusation and confirm the game asks before locking it.
- If correct, confirm all three solution cards are revealed.

RECHECK
- Cribbage on a phone: GO / Score / Continue should remain reachable above the hand.
- Standard card hands should sort by suit and highest-to-lowest rank.
- Cabin home buttons/fireplace.
- Avatar and computer-player selectors.

IMPORTANT
The original 18 room games are synchronized online multiplayer. Family Mystery, Prop Hunt and Birthday Seat are still local/computer test engines and are not yet synchronized live across separate phones/computers.
