BLACK FAMILY GAME NIGHT v2.0.0 - PROP HUNT TRUE 3D ALPHA
==========================================================

WHY THIS BUILD EXISTS
---------------------
The previous Prop Hunt renderer was a custom Canvas2D software renderer. It
stored positions in x/y/z, but it still drew characters and props as flat
billboards. That is why the game could show giant wall slabs, flat characters
that all appeared to face one direction, pasted-looking props, and a camera
that sometimes lost the player.

v2.0 replaces the Prop Hunt presentation layer with a real WebGL / Three.js
scene while preserving the Prop Hunt game rules, family roster, controls,
expanded maps, bots and Family Sabotage.

WHAT IS TRUE 3D NOW
-------------------
* PerspectiveCamera and WebGLRenderer instead of manual 2D projection.
* Real mesh walls, floors, fences, furniture and building structures.
* Textured wood, stone, grass, gravel, earth, metal, fabric, plaid and hay.
* Standard 3D lighting, fog, shadows and room lighting.
* Volumetric 3D scenery props instead of line-art rectangles.
* The exact same 3D prop factory is used for world props, disguises and decoys.
* Full 3D family character rigs with body, head, arms and legs.
* Characters rotate toward their actual movement/yaw instead of always facing
  the same direction.
* Walking limb animation is driven by actual movement.
* Dogs have their own volumetric 3D rigs.
* Hunters visibly carry a modeled 3D prop-zapper.
* Hunter shots originate from the true 3D camera direction.
* Third-person camera collision pulls the camera in before a wall blocks you.
* The local player stays centered/readable in the third-person view.
* Farm animals are actual 3D scene objects.

PLAYER / DISGUISE READABILITY
-----------------------------
The gameplay HUD now explicitly shows:
* YOU + your selected family character.
* VISIBLE FAMILY CHARACTER when you are not disguised.
* DISGUISED AS <PROP NAME> after transforming.
* MOBILE DISGUISE or LOCKED & STILL.
* Hunters see their 3D prop-zapper and current magazine count.
* AREA continues to show the room/zone you are standing in.
* A small player-only map shows YOUR location and facing direction without
  exposing opponents.

HOW TO TEST PROP HUNT
---------------------
After deploying, open:
  /new-games.html?game=prop

Recommended phone test:
1. Choose a family character and start Papa's Farm + Shop Complex.
2. Confirm the gameplay status line says:
     TRUE 3D WEBGL ACTIVE
   If it does, the new renderer loaded successfully.
3. Rotate the camera and walk around another character. The character should be
   volumetric and turn/animate with movement rather than behaving like a card.
4. Walk close to walls and furniture. The camera should pull inward instead of
   letting a giant wall cover the whole screen.
5. Watch the YOU/status panel and confirm you can always tell who you are and
   which room you are in.
6. Become several props. Confirm the status changes to DISGUISED AS <PROP> and
   that your disguise is a real 3D object.
7. Drop decoys and compare them with the real scenery prop and your disguise.
   They should all use the same 3D object family.
8. Lock and unlock your disguise.
9. Play a hunter round. Confirm the 3D prop-zapper is visible and the HUD says
   SHOOTING MODE / hunter mode, with ammo and reload controls.
10. Test jump, sprint, camera reset, joystick, D-pad and camera drag.

IMPORTANT ALPHA NOTE
--------------------
Three.js is currently loaded from the jsDelivr CDN the first time Prop Hunt
starts. The game keeps the older renderer as a safe fallback if WebGL/Three.js
cannot load.

This environment could run the full code/test suite but could not fetch the CDN
module inside its local browser sandbox. Therefore the TRUE 3D renderer has NOT
been visually browser-tested here. The deployment/phone test above is required.

If you see TRUE 3D WEBGL ACTIVE, the new renderer is running.
If that message does not appear and the old flat/cardboard look returns, the
CDN import was blocked or failed. The next action would be to vendor Three.js
inside the project so the app has no external runtime dependency.
