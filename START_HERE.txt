BLACK FAMILY GAME NIGHT v1.8.0-prop-redesign-test
PROP HUNT DELUXE REDESIGN TEST BUILD

WHAT THIS BUILD IS FOR
This test release is focused on making Prop Hunt finally read and play like the approved concept instead of a prototype. It keeps the expanded v1.7 maps, Family Mystery v1.6 improvements, Cribbage phone fixes, global card sorting, avatar packs, bots and the birthday Lodge home.

UPLOAD
1. Keep the hidden .git folder in your existing project checkout.
2. Replace the tracked project files with everything in this package.
3. Commit and push to the same branch Cloudflare deploys.
4. Let Cloudflare deploy normally.
5. On phones, close/reopen the site once after deployment if the old service-worker cache is still visible. This build uses the fresh v1.8 cache name.

PROP HUNT - TEST THIS FIRST
1. CAMERA / ORIENTATION
   - The world must be upright. Floors stay below the player and ceilings/walls stay above.
   - Drag the camera around and use CAMERA RESET.
   - As a hunter, try AIM and confirm the camera tightens instead of flipping.

2. ROLE CLARITY
   - Hider rounds must clearly say YOU ARE A HIDER and HIDING MODE.
   - Hunter rounds must clearly say YOU ARE A HUNTER and SHOOTING MODE.
   - Hiders should see PROP / FLASH / DECOY / LOCK controls.
   - Hunters should see SHOOT / RELOAD / AIM controls.
   - Hunter crosshair should only appear while hunting.

3. MOVEMENT
   - Test the large analog joystick on a phone.
   - Test D-pad backup, JUMP, SPRINT and CAMERA RESET.
   - Walk through doors and around furniture; try stepping/jumping onto reasonable low objects.

4. PROPS
   - Rooms now use a 44-image illustrated prop sprite library instead of relying on labelled boxes.
   - Look for lamps, lanterns, mugs, kettles, pie/bread, apple baskets, firewood, plants, rocking horse, teddy bear, framed photos, boots, books, tables, chairs, pillows, blankets, barrels, fridge, trunks, baskets, milk cans and more.
   - Become several props. Your disguise should use the SAME illustrated artwork as the normal scenery object.
   - Lock as a prop and rotate the camera; the disguise itself should remain still.

5. ROOMS / BUILDINGS
   - Walls and structures now have visible log/plank/fence/metal material treatment.
   - Floors change by area: wood, gravel, grass, earth or water-edge treatment.
   - Rooms receive specific clutter instead of one generic scatter list.
   - Check that kitchens look like kitchens, bedrooms like bedrooms, workshops like workshops, barns like barns, etc.
   - Fixed room dressing/fixtures should help spaces feel furnished rather than empty boxes.

6. EXPANDED MAPS
   - Papa's Farm + Shop Complex: shop, barn, workshop annex, garage, parts room, mezzanine/loft, farmhouse living room/kitchen, basement, silo, fields and animal yard.
   - Camper/Campsite: camper, pine-grove campsites, utility/storage, group campsite, boat launch, dock and woods trail.
   - Acreage/Backyard: garage, driveway/front yard, play yard, orchard/woodlot, goat barn, back field/trailer lane and dugout dock.
   - Farmyard/Animal Pens: tractor shed, feed shed, goat barn/loft, pig barn/wallow, coop/hatchery, silo/grain room and pasture/equipment yard.
   - AREA chip should update while moving between named sections.
   - Mini-map shows your own position only; it must not expose opponents.

RECHECK RETAINED WORK
- Family Mystery: movement-cost markers, grouped notebook, case history, suspect pull-in rule, phone action tray and accusation confirmation.
- Cribbage: reachable phone GO / Score / Continue controls, pegging cards and scoring-combination review.
- Normal card hands: suit grouped and high-to-low sorting.
- Human + computer character/style/difficulty selectors.
- Birthday Lodge home and animated fireplace.

IMPORTANT
- The original 18 room games are synchronized online multiplayer.
- Family Mystery, Prop Hunt and Birthday Seat are still local/computer test engines in this build.
- Prop Hunt is a custom browser 2.5D/third-person renderer. This release makes its camera, HUD, illustrated props, room materials and world dressing much closer to the approved design, but it is not yet a fully modelled WebGL/console-style 3D engine.
