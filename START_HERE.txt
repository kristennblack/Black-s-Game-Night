THIS IS THE PHASE Q MOBILE TABLETOP UX PACKAGE.

PRIMARY CHANGES
1. Skip-Bo is rebuilt as a portrait-first, screen-filling card layout instead of a zoom/pan mini-table.
2. Skip-Bo keeps the player hand, stock, four discard piles, four shared build piles, draw pile, and compact opponent status in the core play surface.
3. Skip-Bo opponent hands remain private and show count only.
4. Skip-Bo uses tap-source then direct destination play: valid targets glow and invalid targets dim.
5. Cribbage is rebuilt around a more believable wood crib board with player lanes, current/trailing pegs, clearer pegging ownership, and visible peg feedback.
6. Cribbage preserves completed hand/crib scoring evidence, including actual cards, starter, point breakdown, and score movement.
7. MASTER_MOBILE_TABLETOP_UX_DIRECTIVE.md is added as the governing tabletop/mobile directive.
8. Phase P1 Prop Hunt flagship work, Black Gammon, standard Backgammon, bots, multiplayer, reconnect, and existing rules are preserved.

VALIDATION STATUS
- Technical/regression validation is recorded in PHASE_Q_PACKAGE_VERIFICATION.txt in the packaged release.
- Real-device portrait visual/game-feel approval is still required before production signoff.

PHONE QA PRIORITIES
SKIP-BO
- No zoom toolbar or board panning required for a normal turn.
- Hand, stock, four own discards, four builds, draw pile, and opponent status are readable together.
- Opponent hand is count-only.
- Tap a card/source and verify legal destinations glow while invalid destinations dim.

CRIBBAGE
- Board reads immediately as a crib board.
- Player lanes and current/trailing pegs are clear.
- Pegging cards show who played them.
- Scored hands and crib show actual cards and point explanation.
- Completed scoring recap remains inspectable after the next deal begins.
