WHAT THIS BUILD IS
Phase O is the whole-app animation / physical-action pass built on top of the Phase N mobile layout fixes and the Phase L three-game shelf release.

WHAT CHANGED
- Shared physical tabletop motion system: stable card/domino identities now animate between hand, table, discard/build piles and trains instead of teleporting between server states.
- Screw Your Buddy / Fuck Your Buddy / Smear: played cards travel into the trick and completed tricks sweep toward the winning player's trick pile.
- Deck-driven games: draw piles/decks have physical press/lift feedback and newly visible cards deal/settle into place.
- Cribbage: peg drops and pegging cards settle more naturally.
- Golf: face-up cards flip into place.
- Poker: community cards deal in and pot/chip state changes settle visually.
- Prairie Pots: chip stacks drop in with weight.
- Last Haven: newly built routes draw onto the board.
- Mexican Train: rack-to-train domino movement uses the shared physical motion system; boneyard feels more tactile.
- Skip-Bo: hand/Stock/Discard cards move into Building Piles rather than snapping; draw pile and cards feel more physical.
- Backgammon: checker flight arc, hit-to-Bar motion, proper pip dice with tumble/settle, and doubling-cube flip.
- Family Mystery: visible pip die with a roll animation, retaining the existing step-by-step standee movement.
- Marbles & Jokers / Trail Trouble: existing path animations retained and combined with improved tactile card/button feedback.
- 3D Prop Hunt / Island Life / Birthday Seat: authored animation clips now phase-match walk/run transitions, use smoother playback-speed changes, and use shorter crossfades for fire/hit/jump/land actions.
- Mobile accessibility: prefers-reduced-motion disables the new repeating/action-detail animations.
