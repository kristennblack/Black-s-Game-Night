TEST PROP HUNT FIRST
1. Open Family Prop Hunt on a phone.
2. Start Papa's Farm + Shop Complex as a Hider.
3. Confirm the world is upright and the background/rooms look like the approved cozy rustic design rather than flat geometry.
4. Walk through several rooms and check that furniture/clutter is dense.
5. Become at least 8 different props, especially: gas can, toolbox, bucket, hay bale, cooler, wheelbarrow, lamp and crate.
6. Drop a DECOY and confirm it looks exactly like the prop/disguise, not a wireframe or labeled box.
7. LOCK and verify the disguise stays perfectly still while the camera can move.
8. Test a Hunter round: SHOOT / AIM / RELOAD and confirm the mode is clearly labeled SHOOTING MODE.
9. Test joystick, D-pad, jump, sprint and camera reset.

VISUAL TARGET
- Rich warm cabin/farm lighting
- Illustrated 3D-ish props with texture and shadows
- No outline-only placeholder props
- Detailed walls/floors/room dressing
- Same art for scenery, disguise and decoy
